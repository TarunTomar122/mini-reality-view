# PLAN-video — Neon Verification (Guard / Verifier)
1. **Depth Anything V2 Metric vs Plain Small licences:**
   - [Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-VKITTI-Small](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-VKITTI-Small) and plain [Depth-Anything-V2-Small](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small) are tagged **Apache-2.0** by the authors ([repo](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2)); [Metric-Outdoor-Small-hf](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Outdoor-Small-hf) has **no licence tag** (and `-VKITTI-Large` is inconsistently tagged Apache-2.0 despite CC-BY-NC-4.0 Base/Large/Giant).
   - **Data provenance catch:** [Virtual KITTI 2](https://europe.naverlabs.com/proxy-virtual-worlds-vkitti-2/) is **non-commercial only (`CC BY-NC-SA 3.0`)**. `Metric-VKITTI-Small` is directly fine-tuned on VKITTI2, and plain `-Small` was distilled from a teacher trained on 5 synthetic sets incl. 20k VKITTI2 images ([arXiv:2406.09414](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09414) Tab. 7; unresolved in [Issue #320](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2/issues/320)). Neither was trained on nuScenes (no dev/exam leak).
2. **nuScenes dataset licence ([Non-Commercial](https://www.nuscenes.org/terms-of-use) / [Commercial](https://www.nuscenes.org/terms-of-use-commercial)):**
   - **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** plus Motional Dataset Terms (which prevail on conflict). Any activity expecting present/future revenue, **including industrial R&D**, requires a paid Commercial Licence (`nuScenes@motional.com`).
   - Non-commercial/academic training and publishing weights/results is allowed with attribution and no Motional endorsement/logo use; published weights cannot be used commercially without a Motional licence (and carry ShareAlike uncertainty).
3. **Self-Forcing / CausVid training cost & Wan2.1 VAE:**
   - **Wan2.1 VAE:** **4× temporal × 8×8 spatial**, **16 latent channels**, `1+4k` frames (frame 0 spatial-only; DiT `patch_size=[1,2,2]` → 16×16 px tokens) ([arXiv:2503.20314 §4.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20314), [VAE config](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B-Diffusers/blob/main/vae/config.json), [DiT config](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B-Diffusers/blob/main/transformer/config.json)).
   - **Self-Forcing (`Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B`, 832×480×81f):** DMD stage = **64 H100 × ~1.5 h (~96 H100-h)**; README `<2 h × 64` or `<16 h × 8` (**≤128 H100-h**, 600 iters, batch 64); SiD/GAN = **64 H100 × 2–3 h (128–192 H100-h)** ([arXiv:2506.08009 §4/App.A](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08009), [repo](https://github.com/guandeh17/Self-Forcing)). **Excludes** prerequisite causal ODE-init (16k pairs in paper) and DMD used a **`Wan2.1-T2V-14B`** real score net (SiD used 1.3B).
   - **CausVid:** paper reported **64 H100 × ~2 days (~3,072 H100-h)** (1k ODE pairs / 3k iters + 6k DMD iters on its internal CogVideoX-like teacher, [arXiv:2412.07772 §5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07772)); the [Wan2.1-1.3B repo](https://github.com/tianweiy/CausVid) runs 64 GPUs (1.5k ODE pairs / 3k iters + DMD) with no published wall-clock hours.
**FLAGS THAT CHANGE PLAN-video:**
- **F1 (Licensing):** Because nuScenes already makes all S1–S3 weights **non-commercial**, VKITTI2's NC provenance does not block this research build — keep `Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-VKITTI-Small` (not untagged `-Outdoor-Small-hf`), drop any claim that it is cleanly commercial, and mark our checkpoints NC + nuScenes attribution. (Falling back to plain `-Small` does not remove VKITTI2 teacher provenance.)
- **F2 (S2 budget & recipe):** 40 H200-3g slice-hours (~17–20 full H100-h; 56 h = ~24–28 H100-h) is feasible **only** because 512×256×17f (2,560 tokens) is **~12.8× smaller** than Self-Forcing's 832×480×81f (32,760 tokens) and our S1 real score net is 1.3B (not 14B). **Plan changes:** budget causal ODE-init explicitly inside S2's 40 h cap, use grad accumulation for global batch ~32–64 (small-batch DMD collapses critic loss, [Issue #73](https://github.com/guandeh17/Self-Forcing/issues/73)), and time the 3-net DMD/SiD step (student + frozen S1 teacher + trainable critic) in S0b on 71 GB HBM.
