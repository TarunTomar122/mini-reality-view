## Agreements

Use decoded boxes projected into the chase view. On the same 120 frames, predicted hulls scored 0.417 and annotated hulls 0.459. This justifies testing placement. The renderer draws silhouettes, for which E3 scored 0.318 against a 0.763 VAE ceiling. Start from E3 10k because it shares the temporal training drives. Its advantage over E1 remains uncertain (E1 minus E3 −0.030, 95% CI [−0.077, +0.013]). [Placement diagnostic and paired results](../PROGRESS.md)

I accept Fable’s oracle-box experiment and equal-update temporal arm A control. Together they answer two questions my frozen-branch proposal left open. Can the renderer draw a correctly specified layout? How much do predicted boxes add beyond training longer on lit keys? Keep DINO and the rays available for missed cars. Encode box length, but verify its relationship to the installed game assets before attributing appearance gains to it.

## Disagreements

Defer the dense grid. Road IoU is already 0.848 against 0.94, and neither proposal shows that detection features improve kerbs. Spend the third allocation testing whether history preserves a real placement gain. Also, a wrong map need not destroy half the correct-scene score. Cameras alone already supply substantial placement. Measure the map’s *incremental* contribution. Finally, BEVFormer exports velocities without track IDs. Logged ego motion could improve propagation on turns while violating the six-photo input contract. [Box export](../scripts/bev_extract.py) · [Camera-only brief](carmap-brief.md)

## Plan I would sign

**Preflight and caches.** Verify checkpoint training-split provenance. Audit recurrent alignment and CAN-bus inputs. Use fixed calibration and causal six-camera visual odometry for relative poses, neutralize unavailable telemetry identically in all splits, and reset at scene breaks. Remeasure hull F1 with this configuration. Cache 2 Hz detections on 2080 Ti/A6000 for all train singles and dense drives, six development drives and the decision panel. Keep the final 24 sealed. Use separate box shards and verify every expected token because existing grid files can skip extraction. Record checkpoint, motion source, calibration, timestamps and six sweep IDs.

Before renderer training, compare 30 seeded E3 train drives with 30 seeded unused official-val drives outside decision/final. Render matched lit keys if needed. Use four frames per drive, identical score ≥0.3, identical projection, and report predicted and GT hull F1, precision/recall and centre errors by distance. Flag possible detector over-trust if train minus val predicted F1 exceeds 0.05 beyond the corresponding GT gap. Match train-map misses, jitter and phantoms to those measured val error rates by distance before fitting. Reserve those val drives for calibration only. The six development drives came from official train and cannot measure this gap.

**10 Hz map.** Associate same-class detections greedily by distance to velocity-predicted centres (2 m gate). Birth at score ≥0.5 or two consecutive hits ≥0.3. Advance in a common visual-odometry frame, decay confidence linearly to zero at 0.5 s without refresh, and reproject each tick. On six dev drives compare with a held raster and annotated hulls by tick phase. If intermediate predicted-hull F1 falls >0.03 below refresh ticks, test direct per-sweep detection there. Stop dense fitting if neither passes. Inspect 20 overlays including turns and exits. Follow the temporal manifest’s 50 ms scoring and 100 ms break rules.

Paint visible hulls far-to-near with the judge’s projection and fixed ego mask. Use four class-group confidence planes (cars, heavy vehicles, vulnerable road users, obstacles), inverse depth, heading sine/cosine, length, boundary and map-present flag. Crop 32 rows, area-pool to 32×64. Use a 64-channel 3×3 SiLU stem and stride-two pyramid with zero 1×1 residuals at four encoder resolutions. Null maps explicitly gate all residuals off. Use identical maps in both CFG passes. Share rasterization across training and rollout. Preserve sample tokens in the singles loader and test save/reload against A with zero residuals.

**Three experiments**, each capped at 180 H200 1g.18gb slice-minutes. Time 100 steps, measure peak memory, and time one cache and rollout shard first. Stop if even 2k updates and required on-slice rollout work exceed the cap. Lock the largest common affordable checkpoint among 2k/4k/6k for runs 1–2 and A. Count render/scoring device-minutes separately. Reuse lit latents and DINO caches. Match A’s seed-0 draws, batch 12 dense (144 drives) + 4 all-train singles excluding dev, style 2, bf16 epsilon loss, AdamW, zero weight decay, 500-step warm-up, rank-64 LoRA and camera adapter at 5e−5, map branch at 1e−4, camera dropout 10%. Use separate random draws for 15% null maps and 10% box dropout. [Trainer](../scripts/train_temporal.py)

1. **Predicted boxes.** Fit from E3 with causal detector maps. Score with predicted and oracle maps.
2. **Oracle boxes.** Repeat from E3 with ScenarioNet boxes at the same ticks, unit confidence, identical rasterization and dropout. Score both ways. Oracle/oracle diagnoses the renderer limit. Only predicted-input scores qualify. Select the passing arm with higher predicted-input F1. Within 0.02 prefer run 1 if both pass.
3. **Map plus memory.** Transfer the passing branch onto the locked temporal winner. Freeze its renderer and tune only the map branch for 2k updates at 1e−4 with the same batch and warm-up. Preserve B/C history corruption/dropout and regenerate C’s own histories with maps enabled. For warm start keep the locked strength. For independent rendering test the same map adaptation without history.

**Decisions and stops.** Use lighting-specific judge caches. Score cold DDIM-25, CFG 2 on 30 decision drives × four keyframes × seeds 0/1. Require all 240 pairs. Bootstrap drives 2,000 times, keeping both seeds together and recomputing pooled F1. Remeasure the lit VAE ceiling. Require Mask2Former F1 ≥max(0.382, half the lit ceiling), gain ≥0.05 over matched A with positive 95% lower bound, positive SegFormer gain, and road IoU gain ≥0 with lower bound ≥−0.01. Full swap_scene (cameras, rays, map together) must score ≤0.05 against the original key. Require a positive lower bound for correct minus map-only swap. Repeat on 30 fixed training drives. Withhold any arm whose train gain exceeds its held-out gain by >0.05. Stop before run 3 if neither qualifies. [Paired judge](../scripts/judge_pair.py)

For run 3 retain the placement gates and require positive paired F1 gain over memory alone. At a street switch retain old history, switch cameras and map, and measure recovery against the donor’s cold F1. Require median lag ≤5 ticks and all signed flicker, recall, drift and ghost gates against their mapped comparators. Two drives with exited cars persisting ≥0.5 s veto adoption. Confirm the locked combination once on the final 24. An inconclusive interval leaves it unconfirmed. [Signed temporal rules](PLAN-temporal.md)
