# Astra · Can simulator pairs improve real drone-view geometry?

**Pilot ScenarioNet on nuScenes training logs. Spend the full training budget only if sim pretraining helps place cars and kerbs on held-out real streets.** The supplied MetaDrive drone frame has crisp lane paint, distant traffic, grassy banks and a red ego roof. Its six inputs offer almost no nearby object-placement problem. The 5k-step real SD preview already makes plausible street textures. Accurate placement is the better reason to buy synthetic frames.

### What the evidence supports

- **Conditional generation.** SynCamMaster trained new camera and multiview modules on a frozen video generator. It sampled rendered multiview video, real multiview imagery and ordinary video at 60/20/20. Adding real imagery improved view correspondence, while adding both real sources gave the best FVD in its mixing ablation. This supports testing joint training. Its proportions address a different task. MagicDrive uses maps, boxes and poses to control street images. Those conditions can serve as independent grading labels here, but its generated RGB cannot establish where an actual car was. [SynCamMaster, ICLR 2025, §4 and Table 2](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/9232d474be0a4e5f1e1bcb0765f17f9a-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [MagicDrive, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02601)
- **Driving transfer.** Virtual KITTI found that training on virtual clones followed by real fine-tuning improved real tracking. Structured domain randomization improved car detection by varying content under road-layout constraints. These are perception results. Our hypothesis is that calibrated, dense chase targets teach where a visible vehicle moves in the image as viewpoint changes. Sim materials, repetitive road widths and traffic density can teach the wrong appearance or layout prior. [Virtual Worlds as Proxy for Multi-Object Tracking Analysis, 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.06457) · [Structured Domain Randomization, 2019](https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2019-04_structured-domain-randomization-bridging-reality-gap-context-aware-synthetic)
- EPE uses renderer buffers and matched patches to reduce artifacts from layout mismatch. CyCADA aligns image and feature distributions for recognition. Neither establishes seven-view correspondence after independent stylization. Start with frozen DINO features, real fine-tuning and a third target-style embedding. The embedding labels the source of supervision. It cannot guarantee geometry and appearance separate. [Enhancing Photorealism Enhancement, 2023](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9756256) · [CyCADA, 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/hoffman18a.html)

### Experiment plan

1. **Choose the scene source.** Procedural MetaDrive is a calibration fixture. ScenarioNet can replay mapped roads and logged actors with a freely placed seventh camera. Building facades and parked actors depend on what the conversion renders. Inspect 12 replays spanning both cities against corresponding real frames. Require correct handedness, junction shape and presence of at least 90% of logged cars within 25 m that should be visible in the chase view. If replay fails, budget one hour to verify CARLA offscreen Vulkan and seven synchronized views on one town. CARLA offers urban assets, walkers and weather, with higher setup cost and different layouts. SHIFT's published rig has five forward/side cameras plus a front stereo mate. Its released RGB lacks our rear and chase views. [ScenarioNet, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12241) · [CARLA, 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.03938) · [SHIFT, 2022, supplement Table 1](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/supplemental/Sun_SHIFT_A_Synthetic_CVPR_2022_supplemental.pdf)
2. **Collect synchronized pairs.** Pilot 64 unique training episodes × 20 frames at 2 Hz = 1,280 pairs. Scale to 400 × 20 = 8,000 including pilot episodes. Reserve eight additional training-log episodes for sim QA. Split by original log before conversion. Sample city/map, turn/straight, near-car count and pedestrian-presence bins in proportion to real training frames. Match day/dusk/night and clear/overcast/wet proportions where supported. Record missing conditions. Compare per-camera brightness and saturation quartiles by condition. For CARLA, use at least four towns and tune actor spawning to the same bins.
3. **Match the cameras before encoding.** Use each log's six named intrinsics and full camera-to-ego poses in repository order, including the rear lens. Replace the test's uniform 70° and yaw-only rig. At a frozen tick render 448×252 inputs and a 512×288, 60° horizontal chase from (−9,0,4.5) toward (15,0,0.5). Hide only the ego mesh and its shadow for the chase render. Apply the real encoder's 32-row top crop. Check RGB channel order and project surveyed sim landmarks to within two pixels in each view. Train on equal clean and phone-augmented input copies using the existing independent crop/zoom, colour, blur and JPEG settings with recomputed rays. Keep target RGB clean. Use unit sim coverage for dense supervision. Save depth-derived six-camera visibility separately to distinguish observable geometry from completion.
4. **Train matched branches.** Extend the two-row style table to three in both arms, preserving real rows 0/1 and reserving row 2 for sim. Use identical initial weights. In sim, train the camera adapter at 1e−4 and freeze LoRA at its initial zero update to limit appearance drift. In real training, update adapter and rank-64 LoRA at 1e−4 throughout. Freeze DINO, VAE and base UNet throughout. Keep the shared learned null set and 10% whole-condition dropout in both phases. Start real training in a fresh output directory with transferred weights, fresh AdamW and the same 500-step warmup as the control. Pair the real minibatch IDs, augmentation choices, timesteps and noise. Use batch 16 (11 lidar, 5 splat), current trust weighting and 0.05 hole floor. A fresh control avoids confounding the planned mid-run change in phone augmentation for sd_run1.

The measured 0.16 s/pair excludes resets, encoding and scoring. Time the first 100 pairs before scaling. C trains adapter and LoRA throughout, so it compares practical recipes, not scheduling alone.

5. **Score placement independently of texture.** Preselect three spaced frames per scene. Use ten official validation scenes outside the final 30 for the pilot. For the final 30, render style 1, DDIM 25, CFG 2 with identical seeds. Project current 3D car boxes and road-boundary polylines through the cropped chase camera. Before viewing predictions, verify visibility and kerb alignment against source images and lidar, excluding uncertain or occluded areas. Blindly mark generated car boxes and kerb crossings at two fixed image rows. Score car F1 by one-to-one matching centres within 12 px in verified regions (misses and extra cars count). Score kerb horizontal error in pixels, charging 32 px for a missing crossing and capping errors there. Report eligible counts and paired scene-bootstrap 95% intervals. Report masked L1/LPIPS separately on lidar and on the available trusted splat subset, with scene counts. The trainer's default style-1 evaluation selects splat scenes only.
6. **Decision.** Advance if pilot car F1 improves by ≥0.05 absolute, kerb error does not increase, and masked splat LPIPS worsens by ≤0.01 on a preselected paired subset with splats. Ensure the pilot includes such scenes or use masked lidar LPIPS as the prespecified guardrail. Shuffle six-camera bundles between scenes at fixed noise as a negative control: if placement survives shuffling, stop. Reject the full transfer claim unless the final 30-scene car-F1 gain clears zero at the paired 95% interval and the kerb and appearance guardrails hold. Compare A at 45k before claiming compute efficiency. A gain confined to sim, unobserved pixels or sharper asphalt does not meet the gate.
