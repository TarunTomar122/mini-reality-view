# PLAN-video: Fable's corrections and decisions (28 Nov). Tags: [est] = my estimate, unmeasured; [mem] = from memory, Neon please check.
**Corrections to the draft**
- C1. S0c tie rule is misquoted. My pre-registered rule was "tied -> lower jitter wins; still tied -> tokens, because tokens are cheaper at run time" (13 ms vs 45-75 ms [est]). The draft says the guide is cheaper; it is not. The rule I now sign is in D1.
- C2. S0c lost three parts of the test it cites: the lidar-depth guide G* (the ceiling; it decides the lidar question in point 2), the wrong-drive control, and "reader-T still improving at 6k -> run it to 12k before reading" (no-early-verdicts rule).
- C3. The VAE facts are already checked (Neon, pivot-video-r1): 16 channels, clips of 1+4k frames, first frame coded alone. Remove "Neon to confirm".
- C4. The lag is understated. Waiting for 4 ticks costs 0.3 s before any compute; with compute I expect about 0.4-0.5 s on a 4090 and 0.6-0.7 s on a 2080 Ti [est]. Name the chunk: 1 latent (4 ticks). The published students use 3 latents = 1.2 s at 10 Hz (Neon), so the 1-latent student is ours to train.
- C5. Both seats raised a per-tick mode (each tick coded as a still; Astra made it the main path). The draft drops it but leaves the lag as an owner decision. Add it to S0a (round trip, flicker) and S0b (timing at 4x the latents), or the owner decides without numbers for the alternative.
- C6. The S0a kill is too weak alone: A's own car F1 came through the SD VAE. Also re-council if the Wan round trip is more than 0.03 car F1 below the SD round trip, or its flicker (W) is above the limit. Add the tiny decoder (TAEHV) to the round trip, since S3 depends on it.
- C7. S0b is missing: an fp16 check on the 2080 Ti (Wan was trained in bf16; NaN / drift, Neon flag), the guide's VAE-encode time, and seconds + GB per training update at the S1 clip shape on the slice (needed to turn hours into updates).
- C8. The S2 / S3 split does not match the published recipe. Training on own rollouts is only affordable with a few-step student (Self-Forcing trains the 4-step causal model directly, with the S1 teacher as the score model). So: S2a causal mask + teacher-forced fine-tune (or causal ODE init) from S1; S2b 4-step self-forcing on own rollouts; S3 = 4 -> 2 steps + tiny decoder + live timing.
- C9. Guide rules both seats wrote and the draft dropped: flat-ground warp under the splat; no old car splats (past ticks give ground-height points only); ego silhouette mask; guide built with the run-time depth net, never lidar; each camera at its own capture time and only frames available at emission time; guide dropout 20% + random holes and token dropout 10% from update 0 (this is what makes the guide-off / tokens-off check valid).

**Decisions on the 4 open points**
- D1. S0c: my reader test is the base. It measures placement directly (mask F1), needs no latent predictor that can fail for its own reasons, runs on 2080 Tis (slice stays free) and gives the lidar ceiling. Taken from Astra: a third reader G+T (the hybrid is what we actually propose; reported, not a gate), every leave-one-drive-out gain must be positive, a look at projected car outlines at the side-camera seams before any fit, and emission-time pairing. About 5 2080 Ti-h [est], readers in parallel, run now next to S0a / S0b (it does not depend on the backbone).
  Reading: G - T >= +0.03 car F1 (CI excludes 0, road IoU not lower) -> guide is the main road. T - G >= +0.03 -> attention is the main road, no depth net at run time, guide shrinks to the flat-ground warp (~1 ms). Inside +-0.03 -> S1 trains both paths and the guide-off / tokens-off renders at the end of S1 decide what goes into the live student. This replaces my r1 line "still tied -> tokens": a tie on a small proxy should not remove either path.
- D2. Depth: Depth Anything V2-Small, plain relative weights (Apache-2.0, 24.8M); scale and shift per camera per tick from the known camera height (road pixels must land on z = 0), so no metric checkpoint is needed. Its outdoor metric checkpoint was trained on Virtual KITTI 2, whose data licence is non-commercial [mem]: Neon to check before anyone uses it. Runner-up, only if the gap to G* stays >= 0.05 after fine-tuning: Depth Anything 3 Small / Base (Apache-2.0; takes the known rig poses, so the 6 views agree in scale, which answers Neon's cross-camera flag) [mem]. Metric3D v2 ViT-S only once Neon clears its weights.
  Lidar fine-tuning is in scope, as supervision on the 144 train drives only, triggered by G* - G >= 0.05 in S0c; then G is read again. Two cautions: Neon to confirm no candidate depth net was trained on our dev / exam drives (the BEVFormer leak lesson); and our own weights are trained on nuScenes, itself non-commercial [mem], so the depth licence is a tie-breaker for a clean recipe, not a veto.
- D3. S1: 512x256, clips of 21 ticks (1 + 4x5 = 6 latents = 3,072 tokens), batch 8, native 4-tick grouping. 512x256 is the scorer's size and the size the live budget was computed for; 832x416 is 2.6x the tokens. S0a runs the round trip at both sizes; switch only if 512 fails the S0a bar and 832 passes, or far cars (> 30 m) gain >= 0.03 car F1.
  Train: new input weights and the token projection in full; LoRA r64 on all attention (self + cross) and MLP layers of the 30 blocks; VAE frozen; umT5 not loaded (one cached embedding of a fixed prompt fills the text slot until the tokens are gated in). Control, only if a second slice is free: Astra's r16 on self-attention Q / V, same data and updates (it settles the one seat disagreement). Full fine-tune only if r64 cannot fit 8 training clips.
  Budget: bug gate (overfit 8 clips in 2k updates, <= 2 h), then one long run capped at 48 slice-h (about 30k updates at 5 s / update [est]; restated in updates once S0b measures it), cheap checks every 5k, one dev exam at the end.
- D4. Kills. No stop on an early check (owner rule): the 5k checks and a wrong-drive swap at 10k only start bug hunts. S1 at 48 h: car F1 >= A (0.4456) and road IoU >= A, ID and W down > 9.4% -> S2. Between A - 0.03 and A -> one 24 h extension (the student copies the teacher's car errors and loses more, so a teacher at A - 0.03 leaves no room; the draft's single line treats kill and pass as the same number). Below A - 0.03 at 48 h -> stop S1, read guide-off / tokens-off and train-drive car F1 (underfit or overfit), then re-council (Cosmos LoRA probe on the same latents).
  S2: S2a 24 h + S2b 48 h, hard cap 72 slice-h, gated under the AGREED bars from 8k-equivalent updates on whole dev drives streamed with own history. Miss at 72 h -> one 24 h debug tranche (longer training rollouts, cache refresh), then re-council; the backbone is not touched before that. S3: 24 h. On one 3g slice the whole plan is about 7-9 days [est].
