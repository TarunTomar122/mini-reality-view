# Astra’s vote · Train a four-tick causal video adapter on frozen A@20k

Each new camera bundle requests one chase frame immediately. Use the last four emitted frames (0.4 s at 10 Hz) to steady cars that are still present. Freeze the E3 UNet, LoRA and CameraAdapter at their camera-only A@20k weights. This gives a first-frame control with measured dev car F1 of 0.446. At longC3@20k, history bought 13.0% smoother motion against A while F1 fell to 0.387 and five-tick ghosts rose from 4.3 to 15.8 per run. Car exits decide whether this design earns its history.

## Architecture and training

- In `mrv/video.py`, keep the 16 spatial-transformer insertion points and zero output projections. Split each temporal block into a per-pixel query from the current spatial feature and projected keys/values from frozen spatial features of earlier frames. Allow only ages 1–4 in the same segment. Add a learned per-head bias indexed by age and a zero-valued null slot so attention can ignore every past frame. Explicitly zero the entire residual when history is empty. Replace the current singleton-frame bypass with explicit read/write/off modes. This adapts AnimateDiff’s motion modules to streaming. [AnimateDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04725)
- Cache writes use a separate frozen-backbone pass with temporal residuals disabled. This makes a past frame’s features independent of older hidden states and the current DDIM step. Four rendered frames can still contain inherited errors. At 10 Hz four ticks cover short occlusions and limit direct access to a departed car. K=4 is a pilot choice.
- In `scripts/train_video.py`, draw two five-tick clips per update from the existing contiguous, accepted train starts across 144 drives. Make one frozen write pass over the prefix frames and one parallel read pass over all eligible targets. Enforce the age mask even when a target is also a later target’s prefix. Freeze feature extraction with no gradients, then train the K/V projections and read-side temporal blocks. Keep AdamW 1e−4, 500-update warm-up, gradient clipping at 1 and bf16.
- Use a deployment-weighted Diffusion Forcing objective. Draw independent target timesteps uniformly from 1–961 with independent Gaussian epsilon. Draw each prefix noise level independently from {50, 100, 200} on the SD schedule, weighting 100 twice. Supply the actual timestep to each UNet pass. Minimize mean target epsilon MSE over ticks with history. Remove mixed clip noise and the repeated clip timestep. Keep 10% whole-clip camera dropout so both CFG branches see their respective history. [Diffusion Forcing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01392)
- On 20% of clips replace the most recent prefix latent at one randomly chosen write position with the preceding key when available. Keep that position’s actual camera tokens and leave every target untouched. This creates a cheap stale-car example. Mild history noise alone cannot represent a confidently rendered ghost. Clean-key teacher forcing never exposes this mismatch. Both interventions are hypotheses to test on emitted frames. [GameNGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14837)
- Full Self Forcing generates its own prefix and supervises the video as a whole. It directly addresses exposure bias but adds serial model evaluations and needs a suitable video-level training signal. Reserve it for a measured gap between good key-prefix predictions and failing free runs. [Self Forcing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08009)

## One tick at inference

- In `scripts/video_rollout.py`, replace window averaging and whole-drive camera precomputation with a tick iterator. Embed the six available cameras and rays. Initialize the newest latent with A’s signed Gaussian tensor reused per (drive, seed). Run SD DDIM-25, eta 0, CFG 2, updating only this latent. Keep separate conditional and unconditional KV rings. Historical outputs are immutable.
- After emitting the frame, apply the SD forward-noise formula at t=100 to its final latent using an independent draw keyed by (drive, seed, tick). Run one batched CFG-branch write pass at t=100 with that tick’s embeddings. Append K/V once per layer, evict the oldest and finish before the next tick reads. Preserve signed offset handling, hard-break resets, restart flags and rollout fields. A camera swap keeps the ring intact so lag is observable.
- Before training, check zero-init and empty-history parity with A. At nonzero residual weights compare cached and recomputed prefixes past eviction and across a reset. Changing every future camera and latent must leave the emitted prefix identical. Separately, the signed dataset uses nearest camera sweeps, which can postdate the nominal tick. For a timestamp-causal replay, select the latest arrived sweep per camera and rerun both A and the candidate on identical bundles.
- A uses 25 batched CFG UNet calls per frame. This design uses 25 with temporal reads plus one frozen write. The backbone work ratio is 26/25, with additional attention, projections and cache traffic. On one 2080 Ti benchmark 100 consecutive ticks at batch one in fp16 against A, reporting peak VRAM and p50/p95 camera-arrival-to-display latency including DINO and VAE, plus sustained throughput including writes. The earlier 22 s/eight-tick cache job suggests seconds per frame. A 10 Hz live claim needs sustained 10 fps and p95 ≤100 ms. A few-step distillation experiment follows only if quality passes and latency misses.

## Budget and verdict

- Time 100 updates on one 48 GB A6000 with peak memory logged. Budget 1.5 h through the first 2k checkpoint including startup, assuming ≤2 s/update and ≤20 min setup. At that rate 8k costs ≤4.8 h including one setup. Recalculate from synchronized timing before continuing. Score at 2k, 4k and 8k with training paused at each gate. Keep all project jobs within 16.
- Own cold means this checkpoint rendered with an empty history at every tick and the same cameras, signed frame noise, precision, sampler and reset metadata. Its output should reproduce frozen A@20k. Verify a whole drive in matched batch layout before reusing an A render as cold. Keep true reset metadata even for the empty-history control.
- Score the six complete dev drives at seeds 0/1 with temporal_metrics (764dd3ec95db), candidate against own cold. Compare candidate first, independent A@20k second, using temporal_compare (fc7ee2b886a5), A-derived k=3 counts, floor 0 and cold_ref B. Both ID and W must be eligible and each must fall **strictly more than 9.4%** on shared transitions. Require **car F1 ≥ A − 0.03** and **late LPIPS gap ≤ +0.03** (candidate-to-key minus own-cold-to-key on eligible ticks 150–169). Report contributing drives and paired drive-bootstrap intervals.
- At 2k inspect aligned exits against the key and cold, five-tick ghosts, and early versus late gaps. A car staying after it exits while ID improves is the copy warning. Stop on two consecutive F1 or drift breaches. If safety holds but smoothness misses, allow through 8k. Advance the earliest joint pass to independent confirmation. Close this pilot if none passes at 8k.
