# Astra · Run the sim-pretraining pilot first

## 1. Critique of Fable’s plan

Fable’s best idea is to pair the real cameras with a replay of the same moment. This removes the input appearance gap. The target can still put a road behind a missing building or a car at the wrong depth. A semantic road mask would reward both errors. SimGen supports using simulator layout as a condition. Wei et al. tested mixing on planar pushing, which leaves the best ratio for chase-view generation open. [SimGen, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09386) · [Wei et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22634)

Fable is right to grade against logged objects and validate the grader first. A car mask spilling across two projected boxes can earn two hits, and two sampling seeds measure little about variation across streets. The proposed pretraining and twin pilots also start from different checkpoints. Choosing between them on those scores could reward the extra real training.

## 2. Converged plan

1. **Verify replay before collecting data.** On five training scenes spanning Boston and Singapore, join keyframes by timestamp. Use each log’s six calibrated intrinsics and full poses in repository camera order at 448×252. Check the expected ~65° front/side and ~89° rear horizontal fields of view against the intrinsics. Overlay ego footprint, moving boxes and map corners on lidar in the ego frame. Check axle-to-centre offset and length/width separately. Require median ground-plane centre error ≤0.3 m for cars within 25 m and calibrated landmark projections within 2 px in each rendered view. Fix or stop on systematic offsets.
2. **Collect 1,280 pairs.** Sample 64 official training scenes × 20 keyframes at 2 Hz, stratified by city, turn and nearby traffic. Render all seven views at one tick. Hide the ego mesh and shadow in the chase view. Render at 512×288, 60° horizontal, from (−9,0,4.5) toward (15,0,0.5) m, then crop the top 32 rows. Use full coverage for sim-to-sim targets. Sample clean and phone-augmented inputs equally with matching rays.
3. **Test the owner’s sequence.** Start A and B from identical SD 1.5, rank-64 LoRA and adapter weights. Give both three style rows (0 lidar, 1 splat, 2 sim). B gets 1,000 sim-only updates to the adapter with LoRA frozen. Then train adapter and LoRA on real data for 3,000 updates in both arms, using fresh AdamW, LR 1e−4 and 500-step warmup per phase. Keep DINO, VAE and base UNet frozen throughout. Retain 10% whole-condition dropout and the shared learned null. Pair real batches (11 lidar + 5 splat), phone copies, noise, timesteps and dropout draws. Use existing real trust weights and 0.05 hole floor. Save A at 3k and 4k for equal-real and equal-update comparisons. Use fresh runs because the planned phone-data switch at 10k changes the running baseline.
4. **Measure held-out placement.** Freeze three spaced frames in each of ten validation scenes outside the final 30. Project current 3D car boxes and road edges through the cropped chase camera. Before seeing outputs, use cameras and lidar to mark verified visible regions within 30 m, excluding occlusions. Blindly box generated cars there. Match box centres one-to-one within 12 px, maximizing matches. Pool counts for F1 = 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN). At rows 160 and 208, mark each verified left/right kerb crossing and score absolute horizontal error, capped at 32 px including misses. Validate on readable keys: shifting annotations 24 px must lower F1 and increase kerb error. Require correctly conditioned renders to beat shuffled-camera renders on F1 at fixed noise. Score style 1, DDIM 25, CFG 2, averaging two paired seeds.
5. **Spend up to 3.9 GPU-hours.** Allow 0.75 h for replay checks, rendering and encoding, 1.20 h each for A and B at the logged throughput, and 0.75 h for evaluation. Advance only for ≥0.05 absolute F1 gain over A at 3k, no increase in mean kerb error and ≤0.01 worse masked LPIPS on a preselected trusted splat subset. Report subset size, masked L1 and paired scene-bootstrap intervals. The low-lidar 23.9/0.348 versus mean-image 30.1/0.615 is context for key quality. On a pass, scale to 8,000 pairs, 5k sim + 40k real versus A at 40k/45k. Require the final 30-scene F1 gain’s 95% interval above zero with both guardrails intact. Claim compute savings only after accounting for data preparation and the equal-cost control.

## 3. Remaining disagreements

- **Twin-key mixing.** I would test 16 real + 4 twin rows next only if visible surfaces in real and replay views agree in the five-scene audit. A matched-checkpoint gain over real-only would justify continued mixing.
- **Automatic scoring.** I would adopt SegFormer once its paired model ranking agrees with blind marks and it penalizes deliberately displaced cars and kerbs.
