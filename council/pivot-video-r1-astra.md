# Astra · pretrained video pivot

## 1. Backbone choice at 512×256

- **Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B** has the best starting point for the causal experiment. Apache-2.0 weights, full space-time DiT attention, causal VAE stride 4×8×8 (time × height × width). Stock 5 s of 480p takes ~4 min on a 4090, with 8.19 GB reported for T2V inference. VACE has a 1.3B control release. The standard 2.1 I2V release is 14B and the smaller 2.2 TI2V is 5B. [2.1 releases](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.1) · [2.2 releases](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2) · [Wan paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20314)
- **LTX-Video 2B** offers 0.9.6 dev/distilled and 0.9.8 distilled weights. Full space-time attention, 8×32×32 VAE, just 16×8 latent sites at our crop. The 0.9.6 distilled release recommends eight steps and reports 15× acceleration over its undistilled model. Test distant-car reconstruction before counting that speed. The linked 0.X licence permits restricted commercial use and requires a paid agreement for entities with annual revenue ≥$10M. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00103) · [Releases](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-Video) · [Licence](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-Video/blob/main/LTX-Video-Open-Weights-License-0.X.txt)
- **Cosmos-Predict2-2B Video2World** offers a 480p/10-fps checkpoint, dense space-time attention, optional sparse variants and a 4×8×8 tokenizer. NVIDIA Open Model License permits commercial use subject to its terms. Its listed 32.54 GB inference footprint and output frame rate give no 11 GB live guarantee. Predict2.5 separately releases a 2B seven-camera driving model. [Weights](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2-2B-Video2World) · [VRAM](https://docs.nvidia.com/cosmos/latest/predict2/model_matrix.html) · [Tokenizer](https://github.com/NVlabs/cosmos-policy/blob/main/cosmos_policy/tokenizers/wan2pt1.py) · [2.5 variants](https://docs.nvidia.com/cosmos/latest/predict2.5/model_matrix.html)
- **CogVideoX-2B** has Apache-2.0 T2V weights, joint 3D attention and a causal 4×8×8 VAE. The card specifies 49 frames at 720×480 and 8-fps playback. Adapting its cadence and building the camera-conditioned causal student are both on us. [Weights and timings](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/CogVideoX-2b) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06072)

## 2. Condition on the six cameras

Reuse A@20k’s CameraAdapter weights and cached DINOv2-S features (6 × 144 × 384). It already embeds calibrated six-component rays, patch positions and camera IDs into 865 tokens of width 768. Add a learned projection and zero-gated residual cross-attention at selected Wan blocks. Each chase latent queries its own tick’s tokens. Cache the fixed style prompt once. [Existing adapter](../mrv/sd.py)
Express rays in the calibrated ego/chase frame. Start with fixed extrinsics. For moving history, test relative pose estimated only from images through the present tick. nuScenes global pose, maps, boxes and sim depth may diagnose errors offline. MagicDrive-V2 motivates explicit camera geometry, though its annotated controls would change our runtime inputs. [MagicDrive-V2](https://github.com/flymin/MagicDrive-V2)
At fixed seed/history, swap the current six images between drives and score against both keys. A newly visible car must follow the donor observation on that tick. If it fails, test a camera-derived depth-weighted feature projection into the chase grid before buying more teacher steps.

## 3. One decision per arriving tick

At 10 Hz, gathering four camera ticks costs the earliest frame 300 ms before compute (LTX eight costs 700 ms). Predicting the whole group immediately leaves later frames blind to new observations. A causal VAE alone solves neither problem.
Pilot one **independently image-encoded Wan latent per RGB tick**, decoded in single-image mode. Preserve temporal attention in the pretrained DiT and retrain on this representation from stage 1. This changes latent statistics and roughly quadruples the steady-state latent rate. Check reconstruction, moving-car response and runtime early.
Use block-causal attention over complete spatial slices, a bounded rolling KV cache of clean generated history, fresh current-camera conditioning each tick and a cache reset per drive. Self Forcing reports 8.9 decoded fps/450 ms first output for one latent per block and 17 fps/690 ms for three on H100 (480p, four steps). Its optimized 4090 demo reports ~10 decoded fps. [Paper](https://self-forcing.github.io/static/self_forcing.pdf) · [Demo](https://self-forcing.github.io/)
**Hardware verdict:** expect >100 ms per fresh tick on 2080 Ti until measured. The project’s four-step SD reference is 310 ms and uses a different network. Benchmark fp16 batch-one on Turing and the H200 slice, then qualify a 4090-class deployment separately. Time six-image encoding, transfers, adapter, denoising and decode from synchronized camera availability. Record cold start, sustained Hz, p50/p95 age and peak VRAM.

## 4. Staged experiment and stopping rules

Budgets below are proposed wall-hour ceilings on one shared 3g.71gb slice. Start at 512×256, batch one, five ticks, bf16, frozen VAE, cached training features and gradient checkpointing. A 1.3B frozen DiT alone is ~2.6 GB at two bytes/weight. Measure actual training peak and seconds/update after warm-up before extrapolating.
- **S0 · 2 slice-hours.** Run stock T2V with a fixed chase prompt and stock Wan VAE round-trips on the same dev keys in native-video and per-tick image modes. Inspect crossings and small cars. Compare reconstruction car F1, road IoU and LPIPS with A. Profile a one-step, warm-cache tick including online DINO. Stop on reconstruction F1 below A−0.03 or if training cannot fit. A measured latency floor over 100 ms closes live qualification on that device.
- **S1 · 12 slice-hours.** Fit bidirectional flow-matching teacher on the 144 train drives, per-tick latents, adapter + rank-16 LoRA, independent tick noise. Use 5→9 ticks if memory allows. At half/end budget score geometry and the camera-swap control on dev. Stop if car F1 < A−0.03 or donor response fails at the final check. Label all bidirectional results offline.
- **S2 · 16 slice-hours.** Initialize causal student from S1, fit teacher denoising targets on matched prefixes and progressively roll out its own outputs. Perturb all future cameras at matched noise/layout and demand identical outputs through the cut. Verify a current-tick intervention still moves the car. Stop on leakage, stale response or failure of the joint dev gate below.
- **S3 · 24 slice-hours.** Distil S2 trajectories at four, then two, then one step, with generated-history training and cached teacher targets. Choose the fastest joint pass. Stop if no student sustains 10 fresh ticks/s at p95 ≤100 ms on the named device with quality intact. Full Self Forcing reports 600 iterations on 64 H100s in <2 h, so these ceilings fund a pilot. [CausVid](https://github.com/tianweiy/CausVid) · [Self Forcing training](https://github.com/guandeh17/Self-Forcing)
- **Joint dev gate.** Six drives × seeds 0/1, complete paired rollouts vs signed A@20k. Require eligible ID and W reductions each >9.4%, car F1 ≥ A−0.03, late LPIPS gap ≤+0.03 vs the student reset every tick (150–169). Report early gap, ghosts, flips, road IoU, paired drive-bootstrap intervals and leave-one-drive-out sensitivity. Inspect ghost increases at exits. Open the 30-drive exam once for the locked recipe. [Signed comparison protocol](causal-video-s2-astra.md)

## 5. When to switch

If Wan reconstructs cars and responds to cameras but misses latency, benchmark LTX distilled with the identical per-tick reconstruction and timing tests. If geometry still fails after the projection test, try Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B/auto/multiview on the same donor test. Its seven road-facing outputs still need adaptation to a chase target. If all candidates miss 100 ms, report the measured rate and hardware.

PROPOSAL: Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B — reuse the six-camera adapter, learn one latent per observed tick, and require a same-tick car response before distillation.
