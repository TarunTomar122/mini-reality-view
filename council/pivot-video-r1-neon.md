# FACT CHECK — Video Pivot Round r1 (Neon)

## 1. Backbones (~1.3B–2B open weights, licences, VAEs, published speeds)
- **Wan 2.1 1.3B** ([T2V-1.3B](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B), [VACE-1.3B](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-VACE-1.3B)): **Apache-2.0**, open weights. 3D full-attn DiT, causal 3D VAE **4×8×8** (T×H×W, 16ch, `1+4k` frames) + 1×2×2 patchify. Stock 480p 5s (50 steps): ~8.2 GB VRAM (T5 offloaded), ~4 min on RTX 4090. **FLAG:** NO official `Wan2.1-I2V-1.3B` (official I2V is 14B only; 1.3B I2V/InP is third-party/PAI).
- **Wan 2.2** ([TI2V-5B](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-TI2V-5B), A14B MoE): **Apache-2.0**, open weights. **FLAG: NO Wan 2.2 1.3B exists!** Smallest official Wan 2.2 is **5B** (uses 4×16×16 Wan2.2-VAE, 720p@24fps on 4090). Do not plan around a nonexistent "Wan 2.2 1.3B".
- **LTX-Video 2B** ([Lightricks/LTX-Video](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-Video), `ltxv-2b-0.9.8-distilled` + fp8, [arXiv:2501.00103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00103)): **LICENCE SPLIT:** code Apache-2.0, weights **LTX-Video Open Weights License** (free commercial ONLY <$10M ARR; paid above). Causal 3D VAE **8×32×32** (128ch, 1:192, `1+8k` frames) + **bidirectional** 3D DiT. Published: 121f 768×512 in 2s on H100 (20 steps, pre-distill); distilled uses ~8 steps. **FLAG:** 8× temporal VAE = 8 frames/latent = **0.8 s buffering** at 10 Hz, and DiT is NOT causal.
- **Cosmos-Predict2 2B** ([nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2-2B-Video2World](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2-2B-Video2World)): **NVIDIA Open Model License** (free commercial, **gated** HF). 3D DiT; uses Wan2.1 **4×8×8** (16ch) VAE (Predict2.5-2B also exists). 480p/720p @ 10/16 fps + NATTEN sparse attn. **FLAGS:** default 720p@16fps needs **~32.5 GB VRAM** (>4090 24 GB & 2080 Ti 11 GB) and **~229 s** on H100 SXM; no published causal/few-step distillation.
- **CogVideoX-2B** ([THUDM/CogVideoX-2b](https://huggingface.co/THUDM/CogVideoX-2b)): **Apache-2.0** (note 5B has a stricter custom licence; 2B is Apache-2.0). 3D full-attn DiT, causal 3D VAE **4×8×8** (16ch, 49f@8fps, 720×480). **Native FP16** (2080 Ti friendly), 12.5–18 GB VRAM (needs offload on 11 GB), ~45 s H100 / ~60–65 s 4090 (50 steps). Older; no CausVid/Self-Forcing checkpoints.
- **Other ~2B note — Pyramid Flow** ([rain1011/pyramid-flow-sd3](https://huggingface.co/rain1011/pyramid-flow-sd3)): ~2B, **MIT**, natively autoregressive temporal pyramid, but older/slower than distilled Wan 2.1.

## 2. Streaming / Causal / Distillation Claims (Verified)
- **CausVid** (CVPR 2025, [arXiv:2412.07772](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07772), [tianweiy/CausVid](https://github.com/tianweiy/CausVid)): **REAL.** Converts bidirectional **Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B** via asymmetric DMD (50→3–4 steps) + block-causal attn + KV cache. Published: **9.4 FPS**, ~1.3 s initial latency on **1× H100** (NOT consumer GPU). Chunk size is 3 latent frames (12 video frames) in the Wan2.1 repo (5 latent frames in early paper ablations).
- **Self-Forcing** (NeurIPS Spotlight 2025, [arXiv:2506.08009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08009), [guandeh17/Self-Forcing](https://github.com/guandeh17/Self-Forcing)): **REAL.** Directly targets **exposure bias** (our exact vpriv failure) via autoregressive rollout + KV cache during training on **Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B** (4-step DMD, 3-latent-frame chunks). Published: **~17.0 FPS** (0.69–0.8 s initial latency) at 832×480 on **1× H100** (~10 FPS on RTX 4090 w/ compile).
- **CRITICAL LIVE-STREAMING TRAP (ALL 3D-VAE BACKBONES):** Causal 3D VAEs compress time by **4×** (Wan/CogVideoX/Cosmos) or **8×** (LTX): `1+T_c·k` frames. True **1-tick (0.1 s) zero-lookahead** output is impossible through an unmodified 4×/8× VAE—you must buffer `T_c` frames (0.4 s for Wan; 0.8 s for LTX), and `chunk=3` latents = **1.2 s** at 10 Hz. Per-tick live streaming requires accepting ≥0.4 s chunk latency (1 latent = 4 frames) or adapting temporal VAE stride.

## 4. Hardware & Feasibility Flags (2080 Ti 11 GB vs H200 3g.71gb)
- **RTX 2080 Ti (Turing SM 7.5, 11 GB) — THREE HARD BLOCKERS:**
  1. **No native BF16 Tensor Cores** (scalar emulation only): BF16-trained models (Wan 2.1, LTX, Cosmos) must run in **FP16** (overflow/NaN/drift risk—verify in Stage 0; CogVideoX-2B is natively FP16).
  2. **No official FlashAttention-2** (requires Ampere SM 8.0+): must fall back to SDPA/xFormers/FA1 FP16 (slower, higher mem).
  3. **10 Hz live on 2080 Ti is DOUBTFUL:** Self-Forcing 1.3B hits 17 FPS at 832×480 on H100 (~989 TFLOPS BF16, 3.35 TB/s). 2080 Ti has ~108 TFLOPS FP16 (~9× less compute) & 616 GB/s (~5.4× less BW). Even at 512×256 (~3× fewer pixels), 4-step 1.3B DiT + 3D VAE + 6-cam encoder + KV cache is realistically **~3–6 FPS** (tight in 11 GB; MUST strip umT5-XXL ~11 GB text encoder). State 10 Hz target on **RTX 4090 / H200 MIG**, not 2080 Ti.
- **H200 3g.71gb (Hopper MIG):** 71 GB HBM3e, native BF16/FP8/FA2/FA3, ~3/7 SMs. Fits 1.3B–2B LoRA/full fine-tune & Self-Forcing/DMD dual-model rollouts (w/ grad ckpt + stripped T5), at ~43% of full-H200 throughput.

## 5. Bottom-Line Verification Flags for Astra & Fable
- **Most verified causal+distilled ecosystem:** `Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B` (Apache-2.0, exact base of both CausVid & Self-Forcing)—NOT Wan 2.2 1.3B (doesn't exist) and NOT Wan 2.1 I2V-1.3B (unofficial).
- **Unverified risks to gate in Stage 0:** (a) FP16 stability of Wan 2.1/LTX on 2080 Ti; (b) 1-latent-frame (4 frames = 0.4 s) vs 3-latent-frame (1.2 s) chunk latency/quality at 10 Hz; (c) stripping T5 text encoder for pure 6-cam DINOv2/ray conditioning.
