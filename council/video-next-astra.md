# Astra’s vote · Give E3 two ticks of camera memory

If C fails its signed gates, train one causal camera adapter. B cut edge-car appearance jumps by up to 23%, yet added 7.6–14.2 lingering ghosts per dev run. Test whether a car visible *now* can borrow appearance from its last two sightings. A departed car then loses its source of reinforcement within 0.2 seconds. Similar-looking road patches can still match incorrectly, so exits decide whether this works.

**Build.** Keep E3’s UNet, LoRA and CameraAdapter frozen in eval mode. Before CameraAdapter, match each current DINO patch to raw patches in a 3×3 neighbourhood in the same camera at each of the preceding two ticks. Use mutual nearest cosine matches only. Add one trainable 384→64→384 residual MLP on the mean matched-minus-current feature, with a zero-initialized, bias-free output. Bypass unmatched patches. Cache raw camera tokens only. Keep current rays, style 2 and the unconditional embedding unchanged. With empty history, require pixel-identical E3 output on paired seeds. Local feature correspondence has precedent in video editing. Its value for chase-view synthesis is the hypothesis here. [TokenFlow](https://diffusion-tokenflow.github.io/)

**Train.** Sample eight four-tick clips per update from contiguous accepted 10 Hz ticks in the 144 train drives. Supervise ticks three and four with the same diffusion timestep and noise per pair. Minimize mean epsilon MSE plus 0.25 times the MSE between the two epsilon-prediction errors. This penalizes erroneous *changes* while preserving genuine key motion. Train only the MLP with AdamW at 1e-4, 100 warm-up updates, up to 4,000 updates.

**Stop or advance.** At 1,000 updates score all six dev drives, seeds 0/1, against fixed-noise E3. Stop if either ID or W improves less than 5%, any signed dev safety screen fails, or an exited car persists five ticks on two drives. Otherwise train to 4,000 and lock. Use DDIM-25/CFG-2, signed offset/reset handling and no artificial reset at camera swap.

**Adopt** only on 30 decision drives then one untouched 24-drive confirmation. Use scorer 764dd3ec95db, compare fc7ee2b886a5, shared transitions, k=3 and signed coverage floors. Require ID and W each down ≥15%, 95% intervals excluding zero over 2,000 paired drive-bootstrap draws. Require nonnegative car F1/road differences, lower bounds ≥−0.02/−0.01, edge recall lower bound ≥−0.02, no increase in ghost growth or five-tick ghosts, and median swap-lag window start ≤1 tick (2–3 unresolved). Against own empty-history cold, require late LPIPS upper bound ≤+0.02 and F1 lower bound ≥−0.03. Retain the visual exit veto.

**Cost.** Allow one A6000-hour for training. At the measured cache rate (22 seconds/eight ticks), 60 drives × two seeds × ~200 ticks costs ~18 GPU-hours per render pass. Budget ~45–60 GPU-hours including swaps, dev recheck and scoring, reusing verified E3 cold renders. Time 100 updates and one rollout shard first. Eight available GPUs should fit one day under the 12-job cap.
