   Use instance masks and the signed 0.3-IoU matcher. Report paired drive-bootstrap intervals, road IoU, mask jitter and the wrong-drive control.    S0c selects a lead input only. At the S1 midpoint compare both inputs, guide alone and tokens alone at fixed noise on the same checkpoint trained with independent input dropout. Retain the second path for a paired car-F1 gain with interval above zero and no road-IoU loss.

[Official checkpoint and licence](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-VKITTI-Small)
