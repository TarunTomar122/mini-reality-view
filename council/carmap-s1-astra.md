# Astra · Car placement in the chase view

## (a) Give the renderer a projected layout

Start with **detected car boxes painted into the target view**. On 120 decision frames, predicted hulls scored 0.417 against game-key masks and annotated hulls scored 0.459 under the same projection. This establishes a usable location signal. The ratio is neither detector recall nor a forecast of image F1. E3 draws silhouettes and scores 0.318 against a 0.763 VAE reference. The practical question is whether a box helps it draw a car the judge can match.

Reuse the bottom-centre box convention, lidar-to-ego calibration and cropped chase projection in `bev_box_judge.py`. Rasterize detections scoring ≥0.3 at 512×288, clip at the near plane, depth-test box faces including the known ego occluder, crop the top 32 rows, then area-pool to 32×64. Encode coverage, detector score, inverse depth and visible instance boundaries. Pooling preserves fractional coverage of small cars. The boundary separates adjacent parked cars without assigning arbitrary ID colours. Boxes approximate silhouettes and cannot resolve occlusion by scenery. Keep the six DINO streams for appearance, missed cars and context. Train on predictions, never annotated boxes or key masks. Inspect recall on missed detections and false positives at phantom boxes separately.

Add a shallow spatial encoder ending in a zero-initialized convolution beside SD’s `conv_in`. Gate the residual explicitly for an unavailable map, including uncalibrated phone photos. An available, empty map is a distinct condition. Use the same map in both CFG passes, retain camera dropout, and randomly remove 15% of individual boxes during fitting. The game supplies the car model and lighting. No mesh retrieval is necessary. Zero initialization follows the spatial-control principle demonstrated by ControlNet. [Zhang et al., ICCV 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Zhang_Adding_Conditional_Control_to_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2023_paper.html)

The strongest challenger adds dense features **to the boxes**. They might locate kerbs or support a missed detection. For each chase latent cell, sample the 48×32 BEV grid where its ray crosses z=0 and 1 m, mask invalid intersections, then compress channels and inject spatially. A two-height lookup is approximate on slopes and vertically ambiguous. Extra attention tokens would add up to 1,536 positions to 865 existing tokens and leave projection to the network. I would spend the comparison on the road signal, where E3 already reaches 0.848 of a 0.94 road-IoU reference.

## (b) Initialize from E3 at 10k

It shares the temporal drive list and has the best observed placement. E1 minus E3 was −0.030 [−0.077, +0.013]. Either could be better on new drives. Freeze E3, including its adapter and LoRA, for the two representation tests. With the residual disabled, the matched extra-training control is exactly E3. This isolates what a cheap branch adds and limits the conclusion to that architecture.

## (c) Feed fresh evidence at 10 Hz

BEVFormer aggregates previous BEV features and predicts velocity. Its decoded boxes have no persistent identities. Temporal aggregation may improve recall through brief occlusions, while threshold crossings can still make cars blink. The upstream transformer uses CAN-bus values for alignment and a learned embedding. Audit the checkpoint’s actual inputs. For six-image deployment, use fixed rig calibration and camera-derived motion only. First benchmark independent sweeps with recurrence reset and dynamic telemetry neutralized. Re-measure hull F1 and inspect turns before reusing any telemetry-assisted cache. [BEVFormer paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.17270) · [Upstream transformer](https://github.com/fundamentalvision/BEVFormer/blob/master/projects/mmdet3d_plugin/bevformer/modules/transformer.py)

Prefer direct detections on each six-camera sweep bundle if throughput and development placement permit. For a cheaper causal path, refresh at 2 Hz, advance boxes by predicted planar velocity in a common frame, compensate ego motion from calibrated visual odometry, reproject each tick, decay confidence with age and expire by 0.5 s. Rewarp any dense static context with the same ego transform. Without reliable motion use direct sweeps or disable stale maps. On two development clips compare both paths against holding a raster fixed, scoring intermediate ticks separately from refresh ticks and inspecting turning, exits and new arrivals. Interpolation from the next detection uses future information. Keep it offline. Use the temporal manifest’s 50 ms scoring and 100 ms break rules, and report measured detection latency separately from offline video cadence.

## (d) Attach memory after placement passes

Fit the map without image history first. Then transfer only its branch onto the locked temporal winner and briefly refit the branch with that renderer frozen. Each tick gets its current map. B/C additionally receive their own separate history convolution, with the signed corruption/dropout schedule and refreshed map-conditioned rollout caches for C. Warm start retains its locked noise strength. Independent rendering needs no history. Compare map+memory, memory alone and map with history disabled using identical noise. At a street switch, change cameras and map together and leave the old rendered history in place. Inspect the first five ticks, missed cars, false boxes and exits. Reject persistent ghosts even if the video looks smoother.

## (e) Three budgeted runs

1. **Boxes only.** Fit the new branch on flat TPV keys from the 144 training drives, style 2, batch 16, epsilon loss, AdamW at 1e−4. Target 2,000 updates. Save the final prespecified step. Verify a zero residual reproduces E3 on the same noise.
2. **Boxes plus warped BEV.** Start again from E3. Match run 1’s targets, updates, timestep/noise draws and box dropout. Select the cheaper passing boxes-only model unless dense features add ≥0.02 car F1 or ≥0.01 road IoU with a positive paired lower bound and retain the other metric’s point estimate.
3. **Winning map plus locked temporal method.** Refit on lit dense keys and matching lit singles with the temporal batch mix. Compare against the frozen temporal winner on identical targets, including cold renders. If independent won, this tests the map alone at 10 Hz. If neither map passed, stop before spending this allocation.

Budget **180 aggregate GPU-minutes per run**. Initially reserve 90 for H200 1g.18gb fitting, 45 for extraction and 45 for rendering/scoring, using 2080 Ti/A6000 for BEVFormer and judges. These are estimates. Time one cache shard, 100 updates and one scored drive first. Reuse compatible caches once, record actual device-minutes, shorten runs 1–2 equally or reduce training drives identically before fitting. Keep the full decision panel. Stop if required scoring cannot fit.

Cache by scene, sample token or six sweep IDs, timestamps, calibration, motion source and checkpoint. Extract predictions and grids together using `bev_extract.py`; check exact token coverage because its existing grid-file skip can omit boxes. Share projection and residual loading across training and rendering. Namespace judge caches by key set and lighting. Reject flipped projections, missing pairs or mismatched zero-map reloads before training.

**Decision.** Use the original flat-key panel, 30 drives × 4 frames × seeds 0/1, DDIM-25, CFG 2. Require all 240 pairs and 2,000 paired drive bootstraps, seeds together, recomputing pooled Mask2Former F1. Advance a map at ≥0.382 (half the VAE reference), gain ≥0.05 over E3 with lower 95% bound >0, and road-IoU difference ≥0 with lower bound ≥−0.01. Swap donor cameras, rays and map together against the original key. Require swapped F1 ≤0.05 and a positive correct-minus-swapped lower bound. Swap only the map as a diagnostic. On 30 fixed training drives report the same gains, precision and recall. Flag train gains exceeding held-out gains by >0.05 and withhold adoption pending independent confirmation.

For run 3 remeasure the lit VAE reference, require half that reference and a positive paired placement gain over memory alone. Apply the signed temporal plan’s drift, lag, recall and ghost gates, and require retained flicker gains against its comparators. Keep the 24 confirmation drives sealed until both councils lock the model for a single joint evaluation. Wide intervals leave the decision open. Each arm has one training seed.
