# Astra’s round-two vote · Train one same-noise causal adapter on frozen A@20k

1. **Window — keep four past ticks plus self.** At 10 Hz the oldest direct read is 0.4 s old. Four slots cost half as much cache as eight. Deeper layers can still carry older information, so car exits need a free-running test.
2. **History noise — agree with Fable.** Use one diffusion timestep per clip and genuine past keys. At high noise the current query cannot read a nearly finished car from the previous tick. At low noise it can use nearer detail to smooth motion. Same-level history scored 85.24 vs 84.45 for clean history on VBench. That favors one trial, while our car-placement result remains unknown. [Context-policy experiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22283)
3. **Attention — take Fable’s causal self-attention with a learned per-head age bias.** Five relative ages suffice. Self gives the current camera-conditioned feature a vote. Bypass the entire residual when the past is empty. This removes absolute clip positions, the null slot and the separate frozen write pass.
4. **Cache — agree on one ring per layer and DDIM step.** Read the past at the same point in denoising, then append this frame’s pre-attention K/V. A single post-render write would put the cleaner image back into early denoising steps. [Live2Diff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08701)
