# Pivot r1: pretrained video model (Fable). Tags: [card] / [paper] = read in a source today; [est] = my estimate, unmeasured; [mem] = from memory, Neon please check.
**1. Backbones.** Our 512x256 frame is small, so the count that matters is tokens per second of video, not the model's 480p headline speed.
- **Wan 2.1 T2V-1.3B**: Apache-2.0, 8.19 GB at 480p [card](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B). 30-block DiT, full 3D attention, flow matching, umT5 text cross-attention; causal 3D VAE 4x8x8, 16 channels, first frame coded alone (clips are 1+4k frames) [mem, arXiv 2503.20314]. Streaming work on this exact model: CausVid (arXiv 2412.07772), Self-Forcing 17 FPS on one H100 [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08009) and about 10 FPS on a 4090 (community figure, unverified), Causal Forcing++ ([arXiv 2605.15141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15141)), minWM = open full pipeline "add camera control -> causal -> few-step" ([arXiv 2605.30263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30263), github shengshu-ai/minWM), MotionStream 29.5 FPS on an H100 with a tiny decoder ([slides](https://iclr.cc/media/iclr-2026/Slides/10006850.pdf)), StreamDiffusionV2 (arXiv 2511.07399), LongLive [mem], tiny decoder [TAEHV](https://github.com/madebyollin/taehv), runtime [FlashDreams](https://nvidia.github.io/flashdreams/main/). VACE-1.3B (Apache-2.0) adds a control-video branch.
- **Cosmos-Predict2 / 2.5 2B**: NVIDIA Open Model License [card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B); same Wan 2.1 VAE (third-party conversion card); trained on driving / robot video, has a 7-camera driving checkpoint and LoRA post-training docs. Against: 720p-native, 32.5 GB listed for 2B Video2World inference ([matrix](https://docs.nvidia.com/cosmos/2.2.0/predict2/model_matrix.html)), FlashDreams lists it bidirectional only, no public causal recipe for the general model; text encoder is a 7B VLM in 2.5.
- **LTX-Video 2B distilled**: fastest, but VAE 32x32x8 ([paper page](https://ltx.io/publications/ltx-video-realtime-video-latent-diffusion)): our frame becomes 16x8 cells (a car is smaller than one cell) and one latent = 8 ticks = 0.8 s. Custom LTXV Open Weights License. Reject for car placement and lag.
- **CogVideoX-2B**: Apache-2.0, fixed 720x480 at 8 fps, LoRA 47-63 GB (two card mirrors disagree) [card](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/CogVideoX-2b); almost no streaming work. Reject.
- **Wan 2.2**: I found no 1.3B; the small one is TI2V-5B with a 4x16x16 VAE [card](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-TI2V-5B). Out of range.
- **Apple FastFrames-S** (Nov 2026; 1.3B on Wan 2.1, 2-step causal): research-only licence per secondary sources (HF tag apple-amlr on [-L](https://huggingface.co/apple/FastFrames-L)). A reference for stage 3, not a base. Unverified.
- **What the VAE means for us (the main design fact).** 4x in time: one latent = 4 ticks = 0.4 s at 10 Hz. Mode A (native): frames arrive in groups of 4; the screen plays a smooth 10 Hz but runs a fixed 0.3 s plus compute behind the cameras, and never uses a camera frame newer than what is shown. Mode B (my idea, untested): code every tick as a still (the VAE's first-frame path), one latent per tick; strictly per tick, but 4x the latents per second, outside what the DiT was trained on, and no temporal smoothing in the decoder.
**2. Conditioning.**
- Keep DINOv2 tokens + ray maps + CameraAdapter (mrv/sd.py): widen 768 -> 1536 and feed its output where Wan expects text tokens. umT5 is then dropped entirely, which an 11 GB card needs anyway. Retrain the adapter; the design carries over.
- Time alignment: latent group g cross-attends only to camera tokens of its own ticks (first + last tick, 2 x 865 tokens, so speed within the group is observable). MagicDrive-V2 does the same for DiT + 3D VAE: conditions are compressed to the latent time grid ([arXiv 2411.13807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13807)). Camera keys / values are computed once per group and do not lengthen self-attention.
- Ego motion (dx, dy, dyaw per tick) added to the per-frame time embedding; Causal Forcing++ / minWM feed camera pose as the action signal on this backbone.
- Rejected for live: source videos as extra in-context frames (ReCamMaster, [arXiv 2503.11647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11647)): 6 cameras would multiply self-attention tokens about 7x.
- Second arm, only if stage 1 misses position: a flat-ground warp of the 6 cameras into the chase view, concatenated as a control video (VACE branch is the pretrained entry). Camera-only, pixel-aligned for the road, cars smear. StreetCrafter / DriveX (arXiv 2412.13188, 2412.01717) show pixel-aligned conditions make video models obey geometry, but theirs come from lidar. Untested idea.
- Transfers little: GAIA-2 (arXiv 2503.20523, closed), Cosmos-Drive-Dreams ([arXiv 2506.09042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09042): HD-map / lidar control, not camera-only), Vista (SVD-based, non-causal [mem]).
**3. Live path.** Self-Forcing family: train the causal student on its own rollouts with a KV cache. This is the published fix for our lesson "own history behaves differently from real history".
- Setup: chunk = 1 latent (4 ticks), rolling cache of about 6 latents (2.4 s), 2 steps, TAEHV streaming decoder, no CFG.
- Budget: 64x32 latent, patch 2 = 512 tokens per latent (Wan at 832x480: 1560). Self-Forcing's 17 FPS = about 26k token-steps / s on an H100; the 4090 figure = about 16k. Mode A at 2 steps needs 2.6k, at 4 steps 5.1k; mode B at 2 steps 10.2k.
- 2080 Ti [est]: about 4-5k token-steps / s, i.e. 0.2-0.3 s per 4-tick group at 2 steps. So 10 Hz throughput in mode A looks reachable, with the picture about 0.6-0.7 s behind the cameras; 4 steps is borderline; mode B is out. A 4090 runs mode A with 3-6x room and mode B at 2 steps. Memory: 2.8 GB weights (fp16) + under 1 GB cache.
- These ignore fixed costs (history attention, DINOv2 on 6 cameras, decoder), so stage 0 must time it; random weights are enough for timing. Wording I propose: "10 Hz on a 4090-class GPU" as the headline, the 2080 Ti as a measured stretch, and the lag always stated next to the Hz.
**4. Plan (Wan 2.1 T2V-1.3B, mode A).**
- Stage 0, about 1 day, 2080 Tis + 2-3 H200 hours, no training: (a) VAE ceiling: dev keys through Wan VAE and TAEHV in mode A and B, scored with the signed scorer against the keys (car F1, road IoU, W) next to the SD-VAE round trip; (b) stock model continues dev key clips, pictures only, informative; (c) timing at 1 / 2 / 4 steps with cache on a 2080 Ti and the H200; (d) seconds and GB per update at our clip shape. Kill: VAE ceiling below A's car F1 or above the W limit in both modes -> stop and re-council before any training.
- Stage 1, bidirectional teacher: clips of 21 ticks = 6 latents = 3072 tokens (a tenth of Wan's native load), batch 8-16 on the slice, flow-matching loss, LoRA r64 on the DiT + full adapter (full fine-tune as the control: 21k ticks is about 35 min of video, overfitting is likely), 10% camera dropout. Gate first: overfit 8 clips in 2k updates, else it is a bug. Then one long run, about 1-2 days [est], cheap 5k checks, one dev exam vs A@20k. Pass: car F1 and road IoU not below A, ID and W below A. Fail on position only -> warp arm; fail on both -> Cosmos LoRA probe on the same latents.
- Stage 2 + 3 together, about 2-4 days on the slice [est; the original used 64 H100 for under 2 h at 10x our tokens, fork README]: causal mask + teacher-forced fine-tune from stage 1, causal ODE init, then DMD on own rollouts, 2 steps. Measure on whole dev drives, streaming: late / early LPIPS gap, lingering ghosts, flips, ID, W, car F1 vs A and vs the stage 1 teacher. Fail on late gap / ghosts after the full schedule -> longer training rollouts and cache refresh before touching the backbone. If 2 steps lose car F1 against 4 -> ship 4 steps and name the GPU honestly.
- Final: exam drives once, paired bootstrap + LODO, latency receipts on a 2080 Ti and the H200.
**5. Risks.**
- The 0.4 s grouping is structural; if the owner wants per-tick response, mode B or a 4090-only target is the price. Owner decision after stage 0 numbers.
- The video prior is real footage at 16 fps; ours is game-style at 10 Hz. Smoothness should transfer, car placement is still ours to teach.
- The student copies the teacher's car errors; stage 1 quality caps everything.
- fp16 on the 2080 Ti: rerun parity checks in the deployment layout (our earlier lesson).
- Phone-photo goal survives: the first latent is a single frame, so a one-tick still mode exists by construction.
- I would switch to Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B if stock Wan cannot hold a road scene at stage 0b or the Cosmos LoRA probe clearly wins on car F1 (same VAE, so latents, adapter and scorer carry over; cost: 1.5x compute, NVIDIA licence, causal recipe to port ourselves). To LTX only if the goal became "fast and coarse".

PROPOSAL: Wan 2.1 T2V-1.3B — camera tokens replace the text tokens, groups of 4 ticks, bidirectional teacher first, then a 2-step Self-Forcing-style causal student with KV cache and tiny decoder; live claim "10 Hz, about 0.5 s behind" on a 4090-class GPU, 2080 Ti as a measured stretch.
