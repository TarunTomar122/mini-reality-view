# Astra · Six-camera conditioning for the chase view

## 1. What transfers from published results

- **Geometric guide.** FreeVS projects colored 3D points into the target camera. On Waymo held-out front-side cameras it scored 20.70 dB / 0.283 LPIPS against EmerNeRF’s 19.61 / 0.330. Its projection ablation lost viewpoint control. Its geometry used LiDAR, object tracks and adjacent future frames, so the unresolved transfer is depth quality in a live camera rig. [FreeVS, Tables 1 and 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18079v1)
- **View-token attention.** Stable Virtual Camera jointly denoises target views conditioned on source views and per-pixel camera rays, evaluated across ten datasets. This makes spatial image patches with target-relative Plücker coordinates a credible challenger. Large-scale pretraining supplies its correspondence knowledge. A new six-camera adapter on 144 drives must demonstrate it can learn that mapping. [Stable Virtual Camera](https://stability.ai/s/stable-virtual-camera.pdf)
- **BEV / 3D lifting.** BEVFormer reached 56.9 NDS on nuScenes test using cameras, with gains in velocity and low-visibility recall. A height-resolved volume or tri-plane can project occupied surfaces into the chase view. A flat BEV needs a learned height/appearance decoder to distinguish a roof, road and façade along the same column. The detection result establishes localization capability only. [BEVFormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.17270)
- **Hybrid.** StreetCrafter combines target-pixel LiDAR renders and reference-image embeddings on driving scenes. DrivingForward separately demonstrates feed-forward, camera-only splatting on nuScenes. Together they motivate a camera-derived guide plus source-view features. Neither evaluates our elevated game view, moving-car F1 or lingering ghosts. [StreetCrafter](https://zju3dv.github.io/street_crafter/) · [DrivingForward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12753)

## 2. Which signal should place the cars?

- **First choice is the hybrid.** Use the rendered key’s actual lens: 7.5 m behind the ego origin, 2.4 m up, 8° down, 60° horizontal FOV, then remove the top 32 rows. Apply each source image’s own resize/crop to its intrinsics. At a side-camera seam a metre of depth error can move a nearby car substantially. Inspect projected car boundaries there before fitting a generator. [Projection used by the judge](../mrv/carmap.py#L10)
- Batch six outdoor metric Depth Anything V2-S estimates. Calibrate any per-camera scale on training drives and freeze it. Lift current pixels at metric depth, transform from each capture pose to the target pose and z-buffer RGB, inverse depth and support. Reduce confidence at discontinuities and where overlapping views disagree. Give the fixed ego silhouette its own mask. [Outdoor metric weights](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2/tree/main/metric_depth)
- The splat gives observed cars an explicit target location each tick. Current source patches can recover evidence lost at occlusion edges or seams. Hidden roofs still need the game prior. Never accumulate old foreground splats. Use bundles available by emission time and record camera age; nearest-timestamp offline pairing can otherwise conceal lookahead.
- Cost anchor: ViT-S depth at 518×770 took 11.5 ms per image on Jetson AGX Orin with TensorRT FP16, about 69 ms for six serial passes before splatting. Batch-six latency on our 448×252 sources and 2080 Ti is unknown. Time the actual resize, depth and projection together. Reuse intermediate depth-encoder features for attention to avoid a second six-view encoder. [Measured configurations](https://github.com/nodarhub/orin-depth-benchmark)

## 3. Backbone and live path

- Encode the five guide planes (RGB, inverse depth, confidence) plus ego mask with a small 2D stem. Add its patch-grid features through a zero-initialized projection at DiT input. Add target-query cross-attention to pooled current source patches plus target-relative rays at one-third and two-thirds depth. Scale its residual by uncertainty with a small nonzero floor, so a confident depth mistake can still be corrected.
- Freeze VAE and backbone weights initially. Train the stem, ray projection and cross-attention with zero-initialized output, then rank-16 LoRA on self-attention Q/V for game appearance. Use the same recipe on Wan, LTX and Cosmos, matching their native patch grids. LTX’s coarse 32-pixel spatial compression makes tiny-car reconstruction from *ground-truth* VAE round trips an early veto. [LTX-Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00103)
- For strict one-tick response, encode each key independently via the VAE’s one-image path and train one latent slice per physical tick, decoded as one image. This changes the pretrained temporal cadence and needs validation. A stock multi-frame latent group creates collection delay or predicts frames before their camera evidence arrives. [LTX-Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00103)
- Start streaming conversion with Wan’s framewise Self Forcing student. Train on its own emitted history, use a bounded KV cache of finalized prior slices, clear at drive breaks and recompute current-view keys each tick. LTX/Cosmos require separately trained causal attention and cached/full-prefix parity. Changing future camera packets must leave earlier pixels identical. [Self Forcing](https://github.com/guandeh17/Self-Forcing)
- Live timing budget, **targets only**: 25 ms six-view depth/features + 5 ms splat/stem + 60 ms for 1–4 student evaluations including attention/cache + 10 ms decode/display. Require ≤100 ms p95 sensor-to-display with capture age included, measured after warmup on 11 GB. Published clip throughput cannot establish that.

## 4. Four-GPU-hour discrimination test

- Compare hybrid against ray-token attention alone. Both get identical six frozen source feature maps, ray coordinates, attention blocks, output capacity, train drives/ticks, updates and seeds. Give the control a parameter-matched ray-token projection in place of the splat stem. First test whether explicit projection adds usable placement information.
- Fit two shallow causal predictors of the cached **SD game-key latents**, using identical recurrent heads and private seeded noise. This cheap proxy lets the signed scorer decode its native format. Budget one H200 slice: 30 min feature/guide cache and timing, 45 min per fit, 120 min paired rollout and scoring. Hard cap 240 min including preprocessing. [Signed scorer and windows](../scripts/temporal_metrics.py)
- Fix six dev drives, both seeds 0/1, same contiguous ticks and noise, plus own cold rollouts. Compare shared-transition ID/W, F1, road IoU, recall, flips, ≥5-tick ghosts and early/late LPIPS. Report paired drive-bootstrap intervals and six leave-one-drive-out results. Inspect wrong-drive guide swaps for ignored conditioning. [Signed comparator](../scripts/temporal_compare.py)
- Advance hybrid if paired F1 rises ≥0.02, every leave-one-drive-out F1 gain is positive, road IoU drops ≤0.01, ID/W, flips and lingering ghosts do not increase, and the late LPIPS gap worsens ≤0.02. Prefer attention on a clear reverse result; missing panel, weak shared support or mixed results are inconclusive. A proxy win earns a matched video-backbone check before full training.

PROPOSAL: Hybrid conditioning with a confidence-weighted metric-depth splat and current six-view ray attention — anchor observed cars in chase-camera pixels and let source features repair uncertain boundaries.
