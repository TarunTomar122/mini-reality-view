- **blocker — scripts/causal_tests.py:61,65,100–110,120.** Two fp32 UNets remain resident during the 25-step, two-CFG-row cache test, then a third is allocated with the old rings still live. The full fp32 K/V payload alone exceeds 8 GiB at K=4 for these feature maps. The designated 11 GiB 2080 Ti cannot hold that payload plus the models. Isolate tests, release models/rings and run the full fp32 cases on sufficient VRAM. The cancelled log does not establish an OOM.
- **fix-before-training — scripts/train_causal.py:103–122,136–137.** Two clips per drive followed by truncation takes eight clips from the first four dev drives. The next-clip donor is from the same drive on **4/8** pairs. Choose and assert eight fixed pairs with distinct recipient/donor drives. Both implementations can ignore past K/V yet differ from A through current-frame FF. Exercise ≥24 ticks, multiple interleaved steps/streams and both CFG rows with distinct inputs. Check nonzero sensitivity to retained history, eviction beyond K on one block, row/slot isolation and exact empty-history parity with A after perturbation.
- **fix-before-training — scripts/causal_tests.py:99–117 (T3).** A private tick loop sees future cameras only after saving the prefix. Its suffix can change via A alone even with a leaking training mask. Also perturb future inputs in one joint nonzero-weight clip forward and require unchanged earlier outputs and changed later outputs.
